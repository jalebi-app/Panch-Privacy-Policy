Panch - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Introduction

Panch is a social media application where users can share videos, photos and other content.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information, profile details, uploaded content, comments, likes and app usage information.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide app services, manage accounts, display content, improve performance and maintain security.

Data Storage and Security

We use third-party services such as Firebase to store and process information. We take reasonable steps to protect user data.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers to operate the app.

Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting us through the app or our support email.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact the Panch support team through the app.
